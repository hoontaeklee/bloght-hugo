Surface temperatures reveal the patterns of vegetation water stress and their environmental drivers across the tropical Americas. Global Change Biology, 28(9), 2940–2955. https://doi.org/10.1111/gcb.16139 Green et al. introduced the ratio of land surface temperature (LST; temperature of the top canopy) to air temperature as an indicator of vegetation water stress. I was particularly interested in their analysis using random forest and the Shapley value to quantify the effect (e.g., pushing to above the mean) of each timestep on the target variable (i.e., LST/Tair).
