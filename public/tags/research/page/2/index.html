Novelty: putting the feedback concept in the discussion Many studies have reported various factors such as SMC, Tair, and VPD, as the driving the global NEE IAV. This study reconciles these conflicting results by introducing a new common label to the candidates. SMC labeled as the direct effect while Tair and VPD had the indirect effect, as they are the spread of the SMC effect via land-atmosphere coupling (i.e. SMC –> LE –> Tair, Rh, VPD).
